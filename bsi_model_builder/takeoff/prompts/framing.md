Take off every steel member on this framing sheet. You are given the page image, the page text, the grid
calibration, and — when the page is vector — the app's geometry pass: each size label it found, the line it
matched, and the line's measured end points in grid feet with the computed length. Use the geometry pass as
the primary source for positions and lengths; your job is to (a) confirm or correct each match against the
image (wrong line, label belongs to a different member, duplicate), (b) add members the pass missed
(unlabelled continuations, "SIM" / "TYP" repeats, members called out in notes), (c) remove labels that are not
members (schedules, notes, legends), (d) assign the top-of-steel level at each end from the plan's T/STL
tags / notes and slope-correct the length when the ends differ, (e) read the connection condition at each end
(cap plate on column, shear tab to girder, moment, CMU pocket, bearing on wall), and (f) record joists, joist
girders and deck as memo rows (count, span, designation), roof openings (count, size, detail reference) and
the connection counts by group. Positions: x/y in grid feet, z = top of steel in feet relative to the datum.
Flag anything uncertain with "VERIFY …" and an RFI draft rather than guessing.
